EVIDENCE RESEARCH — PRIVACY NOTICE

This notice describes the starter app's intended data handling. It is not a
legal certification. The person or organization that deploys the app is its
operator and must review the hosting provider's and AI provider's current
privacy terms and configure the service for its users.

WHAT THE APP KEEPS

- The app has no user accounts, conversation database, saved chat history,
  analytics, or advertising trackers.
- The app does not write question or answer text to its own application logs.
- Each request is processed independently. Refreshing or closing the page does
  not create a saved conversation to return to later.
- Responses API requests set store=false. Moderation requests use the separate
  Moderations API. These settings do not change the AI provider's account-level
  data controls, safety retention, or other applicable terms.

WHAT IS SENT OUT

- The question or task and selected exam options are sent from the browser to
  the server running this app.
- The server sends that text to the configured AI provider for moderation and
  response generation. Exam and research modes also request web search and a
  separate evidence review.
- The hosting provider may process connection details such as IP address,
  request time, and service logs under its own policies.
- The AI provider and hosting provider process data under the policies and
  settings of the accounts used by the operator. Review those terms before
  entering information.

USER GUIDANCE

- Do not enter passwords, API keys, government identifiers, private student
  records, confidential work material, or sensitive personal details.
- A public deployment has a public URL. Anyone who can reach it can submit
  requests and may cause the operator to incur provider usage charges. Add
  authentication, spend limits, and abuse monitoring before shared use.
- Do not treat generated exam practice as an official paper or guaranteed
  prediction. Check the official syllabus and marking guidance.
