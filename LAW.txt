1. PURPOSE

This note identifies legal topics that an operator of Evidence Research should
review before opening a hosted service to the public. It is not a complete list
of laws and does not decide whether any particular statute applies to the
operator, deployment, user, or processing activity.

2. INDIA LAW TO REVIEW

The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Digital Personal Data
Protection Rules, 2025 are relevant to review when digital personal data is
processed. The Central Government's commencement notification provides for
phased commencement. The operator must check the latest Gazette notifications,
commencement dates, amendments, rules, and any orders that apply on the date and
in the place the service is operated. Do not treat this draft as a finding that
all provisions are currently in force or that the service complies with them.

The Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Information Technology
(Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021, as amended,
may also need review depending on the service's features and the operator's
role. Whether the service is an intermediary or has any particular due-diligence
obligation is a legal question for the operator and its counsel; this file does
not make that classification.

Copyright, consumer-protection, contract, cybersecurity, criminal, tax, and
other central or state laws may also apply depending on how the service is
operated and used.

3. OPERATOR CHECKLIST BEFORE PUBLIC LAUNCH

- Identify the legal operator, its contact address, and a monitored legal or
  privacy contact email.
- Map the data flow, including hosting logs, moderation requests, research
  requests, search results, and any operational monitoring.
- Publish a separate privacy notice that accurately describes purposes,
  recipients, retention, user rights, complaint handling, and cross-border
  processing where applicable.
- Review current terms and data controls for the hosting provider, OpenAI API,
  and any web-search provider. The application sets store=false for Responses
  API calls, but that setting alone does not establish zero retention across all
  providers, endpoints, hosting logs, or account configurations.
- Decide and implement suitable retention, deletion, access-control, incident
  response, backup, and security procedures.
- Review rules relating to children and obtain any required consent or exclude
  child users as appropriate to the service and applicable law.
- Review any consumer, accessibility, grievance, intermediary, or other duties
  triggered by the actual service design and audience.
- Have qualified Indian counsel review this notice and the Terms and Conditions
  before relying on them as a public legal document.

4. USER LEGAL RESPONSIBILITY

Users must use the service lawfully, respect intellectual-property and privacy
rights, and avoid submitting personal, confidential, or regulated information
unless the operator has expressly enabled and documented that use. The service
does not make unlawful conduct lawful and does not replace a user's duty to
check applicable law.

5. RESEARCH AND AI LIMITS

Research answers are generated using automated systems and web sources. They
can be incomplete, outdated, misleading, or wrong. A citation shows that a
source was cited; it does not prove the source is accurate or supports every
claim. Verify important claims against the cited material and qualified
professionals. Do not rely on the service for emergencies or as a substitute
for legal, medical, financial, or other professional advice.
